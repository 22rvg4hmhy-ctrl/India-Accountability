- Distinct source-type labels (academic estimates, independent interpretation, civil-society monitoring, judicial records).
- Reconciliation notes explaining what apparently conflicting measures do and do not establish.

## Not complete
- This is not an exhaustive survey of all available research or all 82 promises.
- No new government-wide rating or claim-by-claim final verdict.
- Independent outcome data are lacking for many individual manifesto commitments and other subtopics.
- No automated monitoring or user-submitted review pipeline.

## Evidence caution
The ILO/IHD report is an independent analysis that uses Indian official survey microdata; it is not an independent household survey. The Supreme Court is a judicial primary source, not an NGO or statistical survey. Access Now counts documented shutdown events, which are not equivalent to published shutdown orders.
