# India Accountability Monitor v2.1 · Economy evidence expansion

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Safari; open locally without a server. This edition **preserves the v2.0 and v1.9 UI architecture**. Economy now offers exactly three chart tabs: Trend, Pre-2014 context, Peers. The redundant Before / after tab is removed.

## Data limitations
37 indicators remain the desired taxonomy, NOT 37 completely verified series. The included new annual World Bank series, RBI fiscal and savings data, World Bank poverty revisions and IMF assessments substantially deepen the available evidence. The official GDP base year changed in 2026 and full historical back series is expected December 2026; do not conflate the fiscal and calendar vintages. Source URLs are embedded with each indicator and in CSV.

Data labels distinguish government, independent statistical institutions, independent researchers, review vintage, single-year snapshots, measured outcomes and unknown attribution. No overall government rating is computed from a selective sample.
