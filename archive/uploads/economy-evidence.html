Reproducibility and research rules

Recalculate: Run python recompute_v13.py with verified_comparisons_v13.csv.

Formula: Percentage change = (later ÷ earlier − 1) × 100; percentage-point change = later − earlier.

Method caution: Do not splice GDP (2011–12 and 2022–23 base) or CPI (2012 and 2024 base) without a documented harmonisation process.

Independent validation: World Bank poverty is survey-adjusted, while FDI and consumption originate in official releases. Each row is labelled and links to its source.
